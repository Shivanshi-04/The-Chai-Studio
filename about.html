Roots in the Doon Valley

From Roadside Nostalgia to an Artisanal Movement

Growing up in Dehradun, winter mornings were synonymous with the hiss of tea kettles echoing across misty lanes, the sharp aroma of fresh crushed ginger, and the comforting warmth of unglazed clay cups clutched between gloved hands.

As commercial cafés introduced machine-pressed paper cups and syrup-pumped beverages, we watched this sacred sensory heritage fade. We envisioned a space that honored the generational alchemy of the roadside chaiwala while elevating it into a world-class culinary artform.

Today, The Chai Studio (Chai on Clicks) brings together an authentic collection of recipes — from saffron-crowned Royal Kesar Chai and fiery Adrak-Elaichi brews to hand-pulled cold brews and artisanal grilled savouries.

“Chai is never just tea leaves and milk. It is an unhurried invitation to sit, breathe, and find companionship in a cup of earthen gold.”
