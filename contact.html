Frequently Asked Questions

Visiting The Chai Studio

Everything you need to know before stepping into our warm space.

Do I need a reservation, or can I walk in anytime? +
Walk-ins are always warmly welcomed! We have plenty of seating across our common guild tables, reading corners, and open terrace. However, during weekend evenings (06:00 PM – 10:00 PM) or for special spots like our scenic Misty Window Nook, reserving in advance guarantees you won’t have to wait.
Is dedicated parking available for two-wheelers and cars? +
Yes! LD Tower features dedicated parking spaces directly in front of the building along Mothorwala Road, with ample space for both scooters/bikes and four-wheelers. Our staff is on hand during peak hours to assist with parking.
Can I work on my laptop or study at The Chai Studio? +
Absolutely. We are proud to be one of Dehradun’s favorite work-and-read friendly sanctuaries. We provide complimentary 300 Mbps optical fiber Wi-Fi, accessible charging sockets near window booths and teakwood tables, and inverter backup so your creative work is never interrupted.
Are pets welcome at the cafe? +
Yes, our outdoor mountain terrace seating is completely pet-friendly! We always keep fresh water bowls handy for your furry friends while you sip on your favorite hot kulhad chai.
Do you offer takeaway packaging and home delivery in Dehradun? +
Yes! All our hot chais can be packed in insulated, spill-proof thermal flasks designed to retain piping heat for up to 45 minutes. You can also order directly via Zomato and Swiggy across the Banjarawala, Mothorwala, and Dharampur neighborhoods.
