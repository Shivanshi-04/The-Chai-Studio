CHAPTER I • THE GENESIS

Where Mountain Mist Meets The Morning Tapri.

It began on a freezing December morning in Dehradun. Watching a generational tapri master crush sun-dried ginger against mountain stone, we realized something profound: Chai in India is not a beverage; it is an unspoken timestamp of daily life. It is the pause between exhaustion and renewal, the unspoken apology, the catalyst of lifelong friendships.

Yet, in modern urban café culture, chai had been relegated to lukewarm dispensers, synthetic chai tea lattes, and paper cups that leech plastics into boiling water. The soul had been sanitized away.

“We didn't set out to invent something new. We set out to restore the dignity of slow-brewed Indian tea—served in unglazed riverbed clay, exactly as our grandmothers intended.”

— The Chai Studio Founders

CHAPTER II • THE ALCHEMY

The Four Sacred Pillars of Craft

Every cup poured at The Chai Studio is an exact scientific harmony of four uncompromising elements: raw riverbed clay, stone-crushed botanical spices, high-altitude leaf selection, and patient aeration.

PILLAR 01 • TERRACOTTA

The Unglazed Earthen Kulhad

Wheel-thrown by traditional potter families using fine silt from natural riverbeds. Kiln-fired at 900°C without toxic lead glazes or enamel paints. When boiling spiced tea strikes the unglazed clay walls, it triggers an instant chemical bond, releasing the legendary Saundaapan (petrichor)—the intoxicating scent of first monsoon rain kissing sun-baked earth.

Naturally Alkaline • 100% Biodegradable potted_plant
PILLAR 02 • THE SPICE POTLI

Seven Fresh Stone-Crushed Spices

We forbid industrial tea masala powders, artificial flavor sprays, and factory syrups. Every single morning, whole green cardamom from Idukki, fiery mountain adrak, true Ceylon cinnamon bark, sun-dried cloves, black peppercorns, nutmeg, and Kashmiri royal saffron are fractured fresh in heavy stone mortars. This preserves volatile therapeutic oils that evaporate in powdered spices.

Whole Botanicals • Crushed Seconds Before Brew eco
PILLAR 03 • THE HARVEST

High-Altitude Orthodox & CTC Selection

The backbone of great chai is its briskness and deep malt liquor. We meticulously blend second-flush CTC leaves from premier upper Assam gardens—noted for their rich copper infusion and malty bite—with fragrant Himalayan orthodox whole leaves that lend subtle floral top notes and delicate muscatel nuances.

Zero Dust Grade • Single-Origin Estate Selection local_florist
PILLAR 04 • THE SIMMER & METTLE

Slow Brass Simmer & High Aeration

True kadak chai cannot be rushed. Our decoctions simmer patiently in heavy brass samovars with rich, unadulterated full-cream milk. Before serving, the tea is hand-pulled in long, soaring arcs between copper vessels. This intense aeration oxygenates the decoction, softening astringency and crowning the kulhad with a velvety, micro-textured malai head.

CHAPTER III • THE JOURNEY

The Chronicle of The Studio

How a single brass kettle in Dehradun grew into a sanctuary for discerning tea lovers.

2021

The First Brass Kettle

In a quiet corner of Dehradun, our founders spent four months testing over 40 tea estates and 16 spice ratios to recreate the forgotten tapri taste in unglazed earthenware.

2022

The Potter Alliance

We formalized a direct fair-trade collective with generational potter artisans across Uttarakhand, ensuring zero plastic disposal and reviving traditional riverbed terracotta craftsmanship.

2023

Chai on Clicks Is Born

We pioneered frictionless digital table ordering and express delivery, proving that ultra-fast technology and unhurried slow-cooked recipes can live in harmonious synergy.

Today & Ahead

Artisanal Recipes & A Soul Sanctuary

From 9 signature kulhad teas to artisanal cold coffee frappes, cheesy double masala Maggi, pizzas, and mocktails. Thousands of warm conversations, yet every single cup is still simmered fresh to order.
