More Than Chai. It's a Feeling.

Inspired by India's timeless love for chai, The Chai Studio brings comforting cups, refreshing sips, and café favourites together in one welcoming space. Handcrafted kulhads, slow-brewed tea leaves, crushed ginger, and aromatic whole spices meet contemporary street culture.
